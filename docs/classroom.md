# A five-minute transfer exercise

Open [Which seals need replacing?](https://proofofwork.fyi/#/page-challenge) to give everyone the same three sealed pages. The link works in a fresh browser and preserves existing simulator progress. Responses stay on the open page. Reloading starts a fresh attempt.

## With five first-time learners

Run each session individually. Let the learner work through Basics up to **Sealed pages**, then open the exercise. Before they select **Change page 2 and check**, ask which pages will need new seals and why. Record the predicted set, their first explanation in their own words, and any prompt they needed. Keep the answer hidden until they commit to a prediction. After the reveal, invite them to explain what happened to page 3's own seal.

A complete explanation has three parts. Page 1 still fits. The new text changes the seal needed on page 2. Page 3 still fits its stored inputs, but must link to page 2's replacement seal to restore a valid chain. Record a correct selection with an unclear reason separately from a correct causal explanation.

Then let the learner mine once in the workbench. Ask what a visitor on another device can see and what would bring the learner back. Listen for the distinction between local balances and chain history and the public miner name and verified work. Ask about ranking and experiments only after hearing their own answer.

Use one anonymous row per session: prior experience, first prediction, reason, help needed, revised explanation, local/public answer, reason to return. Report the actual count for each observation and retain contrary examples. Five sessions can uncover misunderstandings; they do not establish a population success rate.

## With a teacher

Ask the teacher to distribute the exercise link and open it independently on two devices. Can they give the same starting task to a class and discuss different explanations without revealing the answer early? Note where the exercise fits into their lesson and what prevented its use. Record observations separately from requested changes.
