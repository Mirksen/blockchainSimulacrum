Privacy policy

proofofwork.fyi · last updated 3 November 2026

Who is responsible

The private operator of this website. Contact for every privacy question: [[CONTROLLER_EMAIL]]

proofofwork.fyi is a private, non-commercial learning experiment. It has no accounts, no advertising and sells nothing.

What stays in your browser

The simulator runs in your browser. Your simulated chain, wallets, keys and seed words, transaction references, completed missions, your miner name and display settings are kept in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. They are needed for the features you use, are not sent to us, and Reset or clearing the site data in your browser removes them. The site sets no cookies.

Delivering the website

The website is delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). To deliver and protect it, Cloudflare processes technical connection data such as your IP address, the requested address and the time. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Leaderboard

Blocks you mine are submitted to the shared leaderboard at api.proofofwork.fyi, which runs on a server in Germany and is reached through Cloudflare. It stores your miner name (a generated name unless you choose one), the block data and the time, and shows them publicly on the leaderboard. Your IP address is used only in memory to limit request rates and is not stored. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR: showing your blocks is the feature you use. Choose a name that does not identify you if you prefer to stay anonymous.

Usage statistics (PostHog)

To learn which parts of the experiment people use and where it breaks, the site sends usage events to PostHog Inc. (2261 Market St., #4008, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA) with data stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Sent are: which actions were taken (for example the page opened, a block mined, a transaction created, a block tampered with, the chain reset), mining timings and attempt counts, changed settings such as difficulty, theme or sound, and sanitized technical error reports without their message text.

Not sent: wallet keys, seed words, names you enter, transaction text, page content or your clicks. There is no session replay, no cookie and no person profile. Each visit gets a random identifier that lives only in memory and is replaced on every visit; location lookup from your IP address is turned off. PostHog receives your IP address as part of each request.

Usage statistics are on by default. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the experiment (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time: choose Opt out in the notice on your first visit, or turn off Usage sharing in the site menu. Your choice is saved in your browser. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, nothing is sent. PostHog keeps the events for the retention period of the project.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR). Write to the email address above. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
